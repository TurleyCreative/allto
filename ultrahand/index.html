    On the plan
    Blocks

    Apartments and other uses: draw a block, then set its use and storeys.

    Block assumptions

    Storey heights by use: residential 3.15 m, workspace 4.0 m, retail and food 4.5 m, community 4.0 m, other 3.5 m.

    Housing mix

    Set the targets from the local plan or the brief. Houses take the parking of their type (2 spaces, bungalows 1), plus 0.2 visitor spaces a home. Open space is compared with Fields in Trust benchmarks per 1,000 people; the local plan's standard may differ.
